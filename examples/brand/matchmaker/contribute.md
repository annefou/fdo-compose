Tell us what you need. A user story says who you are, what you want to do and why it matters, and it becomes part of this space, where needs are matched with the resources that answer them.

You need no account and no name. Your story is published for you by FAIR2Adapt as a public, permanent record that anyone can find, cite and build on. Because it cannot be withdrawn once published, please do not write anything personal or confidential in it.
